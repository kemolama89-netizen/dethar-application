# Critique rubric

Score each area 1 to 5, and give evidence you can point to on the screen. Do not reward effort or good intentions.

| Area | The question | A score of 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Distinctiveness | Could only DITHAR have made this? | Any prayer app with the logo swapped |
| Identity | Does it fit the fixed choices in identity.md? | Off-palette colours, foreign fonts, redrawn logo |
| Sacred text | Is religious text clearly first and fully legible? | Text competes with decoration, or is clipped or truncated |
| Arabic craft | Was it designed for Arabic and right-to-left? | Mirrored English layout, cramped diacritics |
| Hierarchy | Is the main action or content obvious at a glance? | Everything has equal weight |
| Restraint | Is there one memorable idea and quiet around it? | Scattered effects, decoration without meaning |
| Usability | Does it work one-handed, in a real moment of use? | Small targets, hidden actions, too much reading to act |
| Finish | Are spacing, alignment and states resolved? | Uneven gaps, missing empty or error states |

## Verdict
- **PASS:** every area scores at least 3, and Sacred text and Identity score at least 4.
- **REWORK:** anything else. Name what must change, specifically enough to act on.

A direct conflict with protected content or sacred-text rules is a REWORK whatever the scores are.

Concepts shown only as wireframes cannot be scored on Finish. Mark that area "not yet visible" rather than guessing.
