### The 7 CRITICAL findings at a glance

1. **`morning_018` / its Evening copy** (Morning+Evening Adhkar) — the displayed tahlil formula ("...عشرَ مرَّات") combines the Bukhari/Muslim 100×-wording with a 10× count and additional-clause pattern that traces instead to Musnad Ahmad or Tirmidhi 3534 — no single located primary narration supports the wording+count+source combination as currently attributed.
2. **`prayer_005` / `prayer_005b` / `prayer_005c`** (Prayer Adhkar, the three Quls after prayer) — the `repeatByPrayer` count (3× after Fajr/Maghrib, 1× otherwise) is not established by the cited Abu Dawud 1523 hadith, which prescribes reciting them **once** after every prayer; the 3× figure belongs to a *different* hadith about the calendar morning/evening dhikr session, not the post-prayer context.
3. **`rabbana-hab-lana-min-ladunka-rahmatan`** (Misc Qur'anic, cites Al-Kahf 18:10) — displayed Arabic substitutes "هَبْ" for the canonical "آتِنَا" and adds an extra "لَنَا" not present in the ayah at that position. **Already reported to you in a prior turn this session; deliberately left unmodified per your explicit instruction.**
4. **`rabbi-ighfir-li-waliwalidayya`** (Misc Qur'anic, cites Ibrahim 14:41) — displayed Arabic opens "رَبِّ" ("my Lord") where the canonical ayah opens "رَبَّنَا" ("our Lord"); the English meaning's "My Lord" is consequently also wrong relative to the true ayah, even though it's internally consistent with the (also non-canonical) displayed Arabic. **Same prior-turn item — left unmodified.**
