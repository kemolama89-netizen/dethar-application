## How each was verified

- **Second witness:** the hadith-api Arabic edition (jsDelivr, `fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1`) at the same number: the dataset's matn is there word for word (vowel marks aside), for all 50.
- **Narrator:** the name as written in the dataset's own Arabic chain (`isnad_ar` / `text_ar`), not its English `narrator` field (which was wrong or empty for some — see below).
- **Grade:** al-Bukhari and Muslim by the collection itself. The Sunan: al-Albani's grade as the hadith-api edition lists it (Sahih, Hasan or Hasan Sahih), and only where **no** grader listed there calls it weak. The dataset's own grade (Darussalam or al-Albani) is recorded beside it.
- **Duplicates:** none shares a six-word run with any DITHAR data file or the hadith library JSON, and none has 60% or more of its words in common with any existing DITHAR hadith or with another in this batch (a test enforces both, by meaning as well as wording). Where the same hadith is in several collections, only one copy was taken.
- **Text:** the dataset's `matn_ar` exactly — letters, vowel marks and punctuation — with only its invisible direction marks (U+200F) and a stray leading colon removed. No «عن فلان» framing is added to the text: the narrator is shown in the source line («الراوي: …»).
- **Widget:** 13 are drawn whole; 34 longer ones are drawn as a preview that is their own opening, word for word, cut at a clause and ending «…» (the notification and the full card show the whole text); 3 have no clean cut and rotate in reminders and the full card only.

## Data-quality notes on the dataset (for the maintainers)

- The English `narrator` field is wrong or empty for several: al-Bukhari 6777 names "Abu Salama" (the chain ends «عن أبي هريرة»); Muslim 1888 reads only "It has been"; many Ibn Majah / al-Nasa'i rows read "It was".
- Two Sunan grades in the dataset contradict every grader listed in the hadith-api edition (Ibn Majah 4119, al-Tirmidhi 695).
- The matn/isnad split sometimes cuts mid-word or leaves commentary in the matn (examples above).
