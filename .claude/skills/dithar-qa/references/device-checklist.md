# Physical phone checklist

Give the user only the sections the change touches, written as short steps with the expected result. Ask them to report what they saw, and record it as 📱 only after they do. Installing an APK needs one they already have or have approved building.

## Prayer and adhan
- [ ] The prayer notification arrives on time with the app closed and the screen off.
- [ ] The adhan plays at the chosen voice and volume, and can be stopped from the notification.
- [ ] Times match a trusted local source for the current location.

## Audio Adhkar
- [ ] Scheduled playback starts with the app closed.
- [ ] Playback continues with the screen off and the app in the background.
- [ ] An adhan interrupts it, and it resumes correctly afterwards.
- [ ] Reciter names and the notification controls are correct.

## Tasbeeh
- [ ] Counting by tap is correct, and the count survives leaving the screen where it should.
- [ ] Voice counting recognises the dhikr without double counting.
- [ ] The floating bubble appears, can be moved, and its menu opens in the right place.

## Widgets and reminders
- [ ] The widget shows correctly formed Arabic with no line starting on a stray mark.
- [ ] Tapping the widget or a reminder opens the right content.
- [ ] Back from there behaves correctly.

## Mushaf and animation
- [ ] Page turns are smooth, with no stalls or cancelled touches.
- [ ] Text stays sharp and no page is blank after fast swiping.

## Appearance and startup
- [ ] Launch has no white flash and shows the logo, then the Hadith.
- [ ] Each look applies throughout the app, and the choice survives a restart.
- [ ] The launcher icon looks right.
- [ ] Status and navigation bars match the look.

Note the phone model and Android version with the results. Earlier verified evidence came from a HONOR ALT-NX1 on Android 15.
