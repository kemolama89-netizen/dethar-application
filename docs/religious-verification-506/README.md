# Strict verification of the 506 Approved items (started 2026-10-31)

Read-only review: no app content, code or audit decision is changed by this work.

## Files
- `items-506.json`: exactly what the app holds for each item (exported by `tools/export.ts`; re-run to refresh).
- `progress.json`: per-item checks and final status. An item with `status` set is done; resume at the first item without one.
- `batches/`: per-batch notes, in order.
- `tools/`: scripts used (run with `node tools/run-ts.mjs <file.ts>` or `python3 -I`).
- `cache/`: downloaded primary sources (git-ignored; re-downloadable from the URLs recorded per item).
- `REPORT.md`: the item-by-item report, regenerated from `progress.json` by `tools/report.py`.

## Statuses (the user's four)
- **Verified**: every required check matches an opened source.
- **Needs Correction**: the content is supported, but wording, attribution, reference or translation needs a fix.
- **Unverified**: the primary evidence needed could not be opened.
- **Rejected**: contradicted by the cited source, or demonstrably wrong.

Previous Approved status counts for nothing: each check is re-run against an opened source in this pass.
Earlier audit evidence (`docs/religious-audit-progress/full-799/state.json`) is used only to know where to look.

## Checks
- **Quran verse (Wamadat, Saadi):** the shown verse must equal the cited verse(s) in Tanzil `quran-simple` (tanzil.net download), letters and vowels, with the right surah/ayah.
- **Hadith:** Arabic wording vs. the cited collection (fawazahmed0/hadith-api Arabic editions; OpenITI transcriptions of printed editions); the reference number; the named Companion in the chain; the displayed grade given by the named grader in an opened source (Bukhari/Muslim: graded by inclusion; others: hadith-api grade fields or the author's own printed verdict). Wording that the item presents as a quote must be verbatim in at least one cited source (the "complete wording" check: no silent cuts that change meaning; a marked cut «…» is allowed).
  Every scholar the app names in the grading credit must be confirmed in an opened source. If the displayed grade is confirmed by at least one named grader but another named credit (e.g. a Sahih al-Jami' number, al-Dhahabi's agreement) cannot be opened, the item is Needs Correction (trim the credit to what is confirmed), never Verified.
  Hadith numbering: Bukhari = Fath al-Bari numbering; Muslim = Abd al-Baqi (hadith-api `arabicnumber`); Tirmidhi/Abu Dawud/Ibn Majah = the hadith-api standard numbers.
- **Wamadat reflection:** the cited work, opened at the cited verse (OpenITI printed-edition transcription, located with tafsir_api), must contain the point the reflection makes. Adds a claim the work does not make, or the point is correct but absent from the cited work → Needs Correction (attribution); contradicted by the cited work or demonstrably wrong → Rejected; work not openable → Unverified. Restating the verse itself, or a practical application/exhortation, is not an added claim.
- **Saadi Lataif:** the tafsir excerpt must be verbatim in Tafsir al-Sa'di at the cited verse.
- **English:** only where the app shows English (25 items). It must match the named translation (Saheeh International for verses; for hadiths, the translation named on the item). A DITHAR rendering presented as a translation of a source → Needs Correction (translation). Where the app does not show English (481 items), the English is recorded as "not shown" and does not affect the status.

## Access
